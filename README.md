Import Adobe Photoshop (PSD) files into CE.SDK — the browser-based Creative Editor SDK from IMG.LY. Turn Photoshop designs into editable scenes for photo editing, social media, e-commerce, and template-driven creative workflows, so your users can keep building on the PSD assets your team already produces.

CE.SDK is a drop-in creative suite for web and mobile apps: photo editing, video editing, design templates, and headless rendering. The Photoshop Importer is one of several format bridges that let you bring real-world design assets into CE.SDK-powered products.
